MODEL COMPARISON & NOISE STRESS-TEST LOG (26 Nov 2026)
Script: 02c_model_comparison.py | Plot: outputs/figures/model_noise_robustness.png

1. Using 5-fold Stratified Cross-Validation on clean DeepSlice data (Day and Time dropped, numeric KPIs scaled, LTE/5g Category one-hot encoded), all six ML models (Random Forest, AdaBoost, SVM, MLP (32, 16), Naive Bayes, and KNN) achieved 1.0000 +/- 0.0000 Accuracy and Macro F1, consistent with the dataset's discrete simulator rules.
2. To evaluate robustness under noisier telemetry, we added controlled noise (0%, 5%, 10%, and 20% numeric Gaussian jitter plus random categorical glitches) to both training and validation features while keeping ground-truth slice labels clean.
3. At 20% noise, our arrowhead Neural Network MLP (32, 16) sized with Dr. Healy's heuristics ranked #1 with 0.9674 Macro F1, closely followed by SVM (0.9660) and Random Forest (0.9641), showing smooth performance degradation across folds.
4. KNN (0.9541) and AdaBoost (0.9435) showed larger drops, which we hypothesise reflects distance sensitivity to noisy dimensions in KNN and sequential outlier re-weighting in AdaBoost.
5. Naive Bayes dropped the sharpest to 0.8873 (-11.3%), likely because its feature-independence assumption amplifies single-feature glitches when multiplying probabilities.
