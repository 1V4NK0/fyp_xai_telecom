MODEL COMPARISON & NOISE STRESS-TEST LOG (26 Nov 2026)
Script: 02c_model_comparison.py | Plot: outputs/figures/model_noise_robustness.png

1. Using 5-fold Stratified Cross-Validation on clean DeepSlice data (Day and Time dropped, numeric KPIs scaled, LTE/5g Category one-hot encoded), all six ML models (Random Forest, AdaBoost, SVM, MLP (32, 16), Naive Bayes, and KNN) achieved 1.0000 +/- 0.0000 Accuracy and Macro F1, confirming the dataset's rigid simulator boundaries.
2. To test true generalisation beyond those clean rules, injected controlled telemetry noise (0%, 5%, 10%, and 20% numeric Gaussian jitter plus random categorical glitches) while keeping ground-truth slice labels clean.
3. At 20% heavy noise, our arrowhead Neural Network MLP (32, 16) sized with Dr. Healy's heuristics ranked #1 with 0.9674 Macro F1, closely followed by SVM (0.9660) and Random Forest (0.9641), proving they degrade smoothly rather than collapsing from overfitting.
4. KNN (0.9541) and AdaBoost (0.9435) degraded faster because noisy dimensions distort neighbor distances and AdaBoost's sequential re-weighting chases corrupted outliers.
5. Naive Bayes dropped the sharpest to 0.8873 (-11.3%) because multiplying probabilities under its independence assumption lets a single glitched feature wipe out the true slice's score.
